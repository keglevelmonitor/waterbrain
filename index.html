Built-in target water profiles

26 style-targeted profiles carried over from the original app. Click one to load it into the CALCULATOR tab. All values are parts-per-million (ppm) of the target ion.

Unit system

WaterBrain runs its inputs in either US Standard (lb / gal / °F) or Metric (kg / L / °C). You can switch modes any time by clicking the pill in the header. Salt-addition results are always given in grams and milliliters; only the input side changes.

Reset inputs

Restore every input on the CALCULATOR tab to its default value for the current unit system. Does not clear results.

About WaterBrain

Standalone browser app for brew-day water calculations. Two stateless calculators bolted together:

  1. Water Requirements — strike / sparge volumes, strike temperature, and total brew water from grain weight, temperatures, boil-off, absorption, and mash thickness.
  2. Water Chemistry — gypsum, calcium chloride, Epsom, canning salt, slaked lime, and lactic-acid doses required to hit any target Ca / Mg / Na / Cl / SO4 profile starting from RO water.

This is a pure-JS rewrite of the earlier Streamlit-based WaterBrain. Same math, same profiles, no Python server, no install — runs entirely on the device you're viewing it from. Same visual language as FermTrend and the FermVault Brain dashboard.

Source: github.com/keglevelmonitor/waterbrain. MIT-licensed.

Assumptions
  • Starting water is Reverse Osmosis: neutral pH, zero alkalinity, zero background ions.
  • Sparge calculations assume full-volume water treatment (add salts to the total brew water, not just the mash).
  • pH and salt math is approximate. Compare results with other calculators before betting a brew day on them.
